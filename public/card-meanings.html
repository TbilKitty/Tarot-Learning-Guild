Every card has
more than one story.

Browse all 78 tarot cards. Start with a meaning, then ask what changes when the card appears in a particular position or beside another card.

The 22 Major Arcana cards invite reflection on broad themes. The 56 Minor Arcana cards are arranged in four suits: Wands, Cups, Swords, and Pentacles. Each entry below offers an upright reading, a reversed reading, and a practical question. These are interpretive starting points rather than fixed predictions.

MAJOR ARCANA

Major Arcana

The major cards introduce broad themes such as change, choice, responsibility, and renewal. Their position helps you apply that theme to the question at hand.

The Fool

Upright A fresh start invites curiosity and a small, considered leap.

Reversed Pause before mistaking urgency for freedom.

Ask yourself What new beginning deserves a small, practical experiment?

The Magician

Upright Your skills and resources can turn intention into action.

Reversed Check whether promises are supported by action and available resources.

Ask yourself Which skill could you use immediately to make your intention concrete?

The High Priestess

Upright Quiet attention may reveal what noise has obscured.

Reversed Distinguish intuition from an assumption you have not checked.

Ask yourself What could you learn by listening before deciding?

The Empress

Upright Care, creativity, and steady nourishment support growth.

Reversed Notice where giving has left your own needs unattended.

Ask yourself Where is care helping growth, and where might you need some yourself?

The Emperor

Upright Clear boundaries and dependable structure create stability.

Reversed Rigid control may be getting in the way of useful cooperation.

Ask yourself What boundary would offer stability without becoming too rigid?

The Hierophant

Upright Shared values, learning, and established methods offer support.

Reversed Ask whether an inherited rule still serves your values.

Ask yourself Which shared rule fits your values, and which one deserves a closer look?

The Lovers

Upright A meaningful choice asks for alignment between values and action.

Reversed Explore a mismatch in priorities without forcing agreement.

Ask yourself What choice would align your actions with what matters most?

The Chariot

Upright Choose a direction and bring competing impulses into focus.

Reversed Regain your bearings before pushing harder.

Ask yourself What needs direction before you increase your speed?

Strength

Upright Patient courage can accomplish what pressure cannot.

Reversed Treat insecurity with care rather than letting it dictate your response.

Ask yourself Where would patient courage help more than pressure?

The Hermit

Upright Space for reflection can help you hear your own judgment.

Reversed Consider whether solitude has become avoidance of useful support.

Ask yourself What could a quiet pause teach you before you reconnect?

Wheel of Fortune

Upright Changing circumstances invite flexibility and perspective.

Reversed Look for choices within your control during an unsettled period.

Ask yourself How can you adapt to change while staying attentive to your choices?

Justice

Upright Weigh the evidence and take responsibility for your choices.

Reversed Examine an imbalance, missing information, or avoided accountability.

Ask yourself What evidence or responsibility should you bring into this decision?

The Hanged Man

Upright A deliberate pause can open a different perspective.

Reversed Notice whether waiting is producing insight or only postponing a choice.

Ask yourself Would a different perspective help more than another attempt to force progress?

Death

Upright An ending can make room for a necessary transition.

Reversed Clinging to a familiar pattern may be delaying change.

Ask yourself What ending might create space for a healthier next chapter?

Temperance

Upright A sustainable middle path comes through patient adjustment.

Reversed Restore balance where extremes or competing demands have taken over.

Ask yourself Which adjustment could make this path sustainable?

The Devil

Upright Look closely at an attachment that limits your freedom.

Reversed Recognizing a constraining pattern can be the first step toward release.

Ask yourself Which attachment is shaping your choice, and how much freedom do you have?

The Tower

Upright A shaken assumption invites honest rebuilding.

Reversed Avoiding an uncomfortable truth may prolong an unstable arrangement.

Ask yourself What assumption has changed, and what would honest rebuilding require?

The Star

Upright Hope becomes useful when paired with gentle, consistent care.

Reversed Make room for discouragement without treating it as a permanent verdict.

Ask yourself What would hope look like as a small daily practice?

The Moon

Upright Uncertainty calls for careful questions and tolerance of ambiguity.

Reversed Clarify what is known and what has been supplied by fear or imagination.

Ask yourself Which parts of this uncertainty are facts and which are fears?

The Sun

Upright Openness, confidence, and simple enjoyment can bring clarity.

Reversed Let a temporary disappointment exist without erasing what is working.

Ask yourself Where can you acknowledge something that is genuinely going well?

Judgement

Upright Review the past honestly and choose what you want to carry forward.

Reversed Self-criticism or fear of evaluation may be delaying a needed decision.

Ask yourself What deserves a fair review before you move forward?

The World

Upright Recognize a completed cycle and what integration requires.

Reversed Identify the unfinished step before rushing into the next chapter.

Ask yourself What have you completed, and what will you carry into the next stage?

WANDS

Wands

Wands invite attention to motivation, creative energy, ambition, and how an idea becomes action.

Ace of Wands

Upright A spark of enthusiasm is ready for a practical first step.

Reversed Give a promising idea room to develop before demanding momentum.

Ask yourself What first step could give a new idea room to grow?

Two of Wands

Upright Compare possible directions and plan beyond the familiar.

Reversed Fear of uncertainty may be keeping the horizon unnecessarily small.

Ask yourself Which opportunity would expand your horizon without ignoring the plan?

Three of Wands

Upright Preparation creates room to expand and respond to opportunity.

Reversed Revisit expectations when progress depends on factors outside your control.

Ask yourself What preparations would help you respond to an opening?

Four of Wands

Upright Shared milestones and a stable base deserve recognition.

Reversed Check whether belonging and celebration are being supported in practice.

Ask yourself What achievement or gathering deserves recognition?

Five of Wands

Upright Different approaches can sharpen an idea if disagreement stays constructive.

Reversed Resolve unproductive competition or an issue being avoided for superficial peace.

Ask yourself Which disagreement could become useful if everyone names the issue?

Six of Wands

Upright Receive recognition while remembering the work that earned it.

Reversed Separate your own standards from dependence on external approval.

Ask yourself How can you receive recognition without depending on it?

Seven of Wands

Upright Protect a position or boundary that matters to you.

Reversed Choose which demands deserve your energy instead of defending every front.

Ask yourself What boundary deserves defending, and what argument can you leave alone?

Eight of Wands

Upright Movement and communication may call for a timely response.

Reversed Allow for delays and clarify messages before acting on them.

Ask yourself What message needs a timely answer, and what needs more clarity?

Nine of Wands

Upright Experience has made you resilient; protect the energy you have left.

Reversed Notice when vigilance becomes exhaustion or resistance to all support.

Ask yourself What experience has made you resilient, and where do you need rest?

Ten of Wands

Upright A heavy load asks for realistic limits and shared responsibility.

Reversed Put down a burden that no longer needs to belong entirely to you.

Ask yourself Which task can be shared, delayed, or set down?

Page of Wands

Upright Explore a new interest with curiosity and willingness to learn.

Reversed Turn scattered enthusiasm into one manageable experiment.

Ask yourself What could you learn by exploring before committing?

Knight of Wands

Upright Bold initiative can create momentum when directed with care.

Reversed Slow an impulsive response and follow through before chasing novelty.

Ask yourself How could bold energy be paired with a realistic follow-through?

Queen of Wands

Upright Warm confidence lets you take up space and encourage others.

Reversed Reconnect with your own confidence rather than comparing yourself with others.

Ask yourself Where could your confidence create room for someone else?

King of Wands

Upright A clear vision works best when paired with responsible leadership.

Reversed Check whether impatience or pride is overriding collaboration.

Ask yourself What vision could you communicate more clearly and collaboratively?

CUPS

Cups

Cups often bring feelings, connection, care, and emotional expectations into a reading.

Ace of Cups

Upright Emotional openness can create space for connection and renewal.

Reversed Attend to your emotional capacity before offering more of yourself.

Ask yourself What feeling is ready to be expressed with care?

Two of Cups

Upright Mutual care grows through reciprocity and honest exchange.

Reversed Address unequal effort or a difference in expectations directly.

Ask yourself Is the exchange genuinely mutual, and what conversation might help?

Three of Cups

Upright Friendship and shared joy can strengthen your support system.

Reversed Consider boundaries where group expectations obscure your own needs.

Ask yourself Which friendships or shared joys support you right now?

Four of Cups

Upright A pause can help you distinguish disinterest from emotional fatigue.

Reversed Renewed attention may reveal an option you previously overlooked.

Ask yourself Does your lack of interest signal a mismatch, or a need to pause?

Five of Cups

Upright Acknowledge disappointment while noticing what remains available.

Reversed Allow recovery without requiring yourself to erase what hurt.

Ask yourself What remains available alongside a real disappointment?

Six of Cups

Upright Familiarity and past experience can offer comfort and perspective.

Reversed Check whether nostalgia is filtering out facts that matter now.

Ask yourself What does nostalgia illuminate, and what might it conceal?

Seven of Cups

Upright Many possibilities call for a clear standard of choice.

Reversed Bring a vague wish into focus with facts and one realistic next step.

Ask yourself Which possibility survives a careful look at the facts?

Eight of Cups

Upright Leaving an unfulfilling pattern can honor a deeper need.

Reversed Explore whether staying reflects commitment, fear, or unfinished reflection.

Ask yourself What deeper need might explain a desire to leave?

Nine of Cups

Upright Appreciate satisfaction and define what enough looks like for you.

Reversed Ask whether an appealing outcome would meet the need underneath it.

Ask yourself Would the outcome you want satisfy the need beneath it?

Ten of Cups

Upright Shared well-being depends on everyday care and aligned values.

Reversed Compare the ideal picture with how the relationship or community actually feels.

Ask yourself What does everyday care contribute to your idea of belonging?

Page of Cups

Upright A gentle expression of feeling or creativity may open a conversation.

Reversed Give tender feelings time and avoid reading certainty into a small gesture.

Ask yourself What gentle feeling or creative thought deserves a careful response?

Knight of Cups

Upright Approach an emotional aim with sincerity and thoughtful expression.

Reversed Check whether an attractive promise has consistent action behind it.

Ask yourself What would consistent action add to an appealing emotional offer?

Queen of Cups

Upright Compassion and emotional awareness can guide a grounded response.

Reversed Protect your boundaries when absorbing other people's feelings.

Ask yourself How can you show compassion while keeping your own boundary?

King of Cups

Upright Steady emotional leadership makes space for feeling without losing perspective.

Reversed Notice emotional avoidance or control and return to honest self-regulation.

Ask yourself What would steady emotional honesty look like in this situation?

SWORDS

Swords

Swords bring thought, communication, conflict, and clarity into focus.

Ace of Swords

Upright A clear question or honest conversation can cut through confusion.

Reversed Check the evidence and define terms before treating an idea as settled.

Ask yourself What question could cut through confusion?

Two of Swords

Upright A difficult choice needs space for both facts and feelings.

Reversed Bring an avoided decision into the open in manageable steps.

Ask yourself Which fact is missing from the choice you are avoiding?

Three of Swords

Upright Pain deserves acknowledgment and a truthful account of what happened.

Reversed Recovery may involve releasing a repeated story while respecting the hurt.

Ask yourself What hurt needs acknowledgment before repair is possible?

Four of Swords

Upright Rest and distance can restore perspective.

Reversed Return gradually if rest has helped; reassess if depletion is continuing.

Ask yourself What might rest clarify that more analysis cannot?

Five of Swords

Upright Consider the relational cost of winning a conflict.

Reversed Look for repair, accountability, or a clean exit from an unproductive dispute.

Ask yourself Is winning this disagreement worth its relational cost?

Six of Swords

Upright A gradual transition can move you toward calmer conditions.

Reversed Identify what is making a needed transition difficult to complete.

Ask yourself What would make a transition feel safer or more complete?

Seven of Swords

Upright Strategy and discretion call for an honest check of motives.

Reversed Clarify avoidance or incomplete disclosure rather than assuming deception.

Ask yourself Where is discretion useful, and where might it become avoidance?

Eight of Swords

Upright Question whether perceived limits leave any small, workable choice.

Reversed Recognizing an available choice can loosen a sense of being trapped.

Ask yourself What small choice remains available inside a difficult situation?

Nine of Swords

Upright Worry may need support and perspective instead of more solitary analysis.

Reversed Sharing a worry can help; do not dismiss distress that still needs care.

Ask yourself Who could help you put a worry into perspective?

Ten of Swords

Upright An exhausted pattern may have reached its stopping point.

Reversed Recovery starts with small steps after a difficult ending.

Ask yourself What exhausted pattern deserves a clear stopping point?

Page of Swords

Upright Curiosity is useful when questions are careful and evidence matters.

Reversed Avoid overinterpreting fragments, rumors, or repeated checking.

Ask yourself What can you verify before drawing a conclusion?

Knight of Swords

Upright Direct action can help when the goal and facts are clear.

Reversed Slow down before urgency turns communication into unnecessary conflict.

Ask yourself Would slowing down change the way your message lands?

Queen of Swords

Upright Clear standards and direct language can protect what matters.

Reversed Keep a boundary without letting disappointment determine every judgment.

Ask yourself How can you state a firm boundary without closing off useful information?

King of Swords

Upright Use reason, consistency, and fair standards to make a decision.

Reversed Check whether authority or clever reasoning is crowding out fairness.

Ask yourself What standard would be fair to everyone involved?

PENTACLES

Pentacles

Pentacles ask about work, resources, daily practices, and practical stability.

Ace of Pentacles

Upright A practical opportunity needs attention and a sustainable first step.

Reversed Examine the terms and resources before assuming an opportunity is workable.

Ask yourself What practical first step would help you test an opportunity?

Two of Pentacles

Upright Balance competing responsibilities with realistic priorities.

Reversed Reduce overload by deciding what can wait or be simplified.

Ask yourself Which responsibility needs priority this week?

Three of Pentacles

Upright Good work grows through complementary skills and constructive feedback.

Reversed Clarify responsibilities and standards where teamwork is breaking down.

Ask yourself What specific feedback would improve the shared work?

Four of Pentacles

Upright Protecting resources can support stability when the limits are proportionate.

Reversed Reconsider a grip that is too tight, or spending that weakens your foundation.

Ask yourself Is protecting your resources providing stability or narrowing your options?

Five of Pentacles

Upright Material strain or exclusion calls for practical support.

Reversed Look for available assistance and a realistic path toward greater stability.

Ask yourself What material support or assistance is available to you?

Six of Pentacles

Upright Fair exchange and thoughtful support can strengthen security.

Reversed Examine unequal terms or help that carries hidden expectations.

Ask yourself Are the terms of giving and receiving clear and fair?

Seven of Pentacles

Upright Review the return on your effort before deciding what to continue.

Reversed Reassess an investment of time or resources that is not meeting its purpose.

Ask yourself What return are you getting on your time and effort?

Eight of Pentacles

Upright Consistent practice builds competence and dependable progress.

Reversed Adjust the method if perfectionism or repetition has stopped producing learning.

Ask yourself What deliberate practice would strengthen this skill?

Nine of Pentacles

Upright Recognize the autonomy and comfort built through sustained effort.

Reversed Review whether appearances of success match actual security and independence.

Ask yourself Does your sense of independence match your actual resources?

Ten of Pentacles

Upright Long-term stability depends on shared commitments and durable systems.

Reversed Examine strains in a shared financial or family structure.

Ask yourself What would make a shared plan durable over time?

Page of Pentacles

Upright A practical learning opportunity rewards preparation and curiosity.

Reversed Turn a distant goal into a specific task you can complete.

Ask yourself What can you study or do today to advance a long-term goal?

Knight of Pentacles

Upright Reliable, patient effort can support a durable result.

Reversed Refresh a routine that has become stagnant or too inflexible.

Ask yourself Which routine supports progress, and which one needs updating?

Queen of Pentacles

Upright Practical care includes tending to your own resources and comfort.

Reversed Rebalance care work when your time, energy, or security is being depleted.

Ask yourself How can you include your own resources in the care you provide?

King of Pentacles

Upright Responsible stewardship can turn resources into lasting stability.

Reversed Check whether status, possession, or control is displacing sound stewardship.

Ask yourself What would responsible stewardship look like here?
